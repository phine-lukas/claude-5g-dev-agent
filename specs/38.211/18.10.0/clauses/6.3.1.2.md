#### 6.3.1.2 Modulation

For each codeword $q$, the block of scrambled bits ${\widetilde{b}}^{(q)}(0),\ldots,{\widetilde{b}}^{(q)}\left( M_{\text{bit}}^{(q)} - 1 \right)$ shall be modulated as described in clause 5.1 using one of the modulation schemes in Table 6.3.1.2-1, resulting in a block of complex-valued modulation symbols $d^{(q)}(0),\ldots,d^{(q)}\left( M_{\text{symb}}^{(q)} - 1 \right)$.
