#### 9.3.1.265 PC5 RLC Channel ID

This IE uniquely identifies a PC5 Relay RLC channel for a L2 U2N Remote UE, or a L2 U2N Relay UE, or a L2 U2U Remote UE, or L2 U2U Relay UE.
