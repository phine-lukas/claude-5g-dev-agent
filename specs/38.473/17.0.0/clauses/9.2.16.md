### 9.2.16 QoE Information Transfer messages

#### 9.2.16.1 QOE INFORMATION TRANSFER 

This message is sent by a gNB-CU to a gNB-DU, to indicate information related to RAN visible QoE.

Direction: gNB-CU gNB-DU.
