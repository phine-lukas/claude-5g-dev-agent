### 9.2.13 Broadcast Context Management messages

#### 9.2.13.1 BROADCAST CONTEXT SETUP REQUEST

This message is sent by the gNB-CU to request the setup of a Broadcast context, and establish an MBS-service-associated logical F1-connection.

Direction: gNB-CU gNB-DU.
