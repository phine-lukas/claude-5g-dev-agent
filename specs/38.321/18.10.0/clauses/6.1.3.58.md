#### 6.1.3.58 BFD-RS Indication MAC CE

The BFD-RS Indication MAC CE is identified by a MAC subheader with eLCID as specified in Table 6.2.1-1b. It has a variable size, and includes a BFD-RS-ID<sub>0</sub> field and a BFD-RS-ID<sub>1</sub> field (optional) of ***failureDetectionSet1***, and a BFD-RS-ID<sub>0</sub> field and a BFD-RS-ID<sub>1</sub> field (optional) of ***failureDetectionSet2***.

It has a variable size consisting of following fields:

\- Serving Cell ID: This field indicates the identity of the Serving Cell for which the MAC CE applies. The length of the field is 5 bits;

\- BWP ID: This field indicates a DL BWP for which the MAC CE applies as the codepoint of the DCI *bandwidth part indicator* field as specified in TS 38.213 \[6\]. The length of the BWP ID field is 2 bits;

\- S: This field indicates the presence of the octet containing the BFD-RS ID<sub>1</sub> of the same BFD-RS set. The S field set to 1 indicates that the octet containing BFD-RS ID<sub>1</sub> is included. The S field set to 0 indicates that the octet containing the BFD-RS ID<sub>1</sub> of the same BFD-RS set is not included;

\- BFD-RS ID<sub>i</sub>: This field indicates the BFD-RS resource from ***failureDetectionSet1*** or ***failureDetectionSet2*** as specified in TS 38.331 \[5\];

\- R: Reserved bit, set to 0.
