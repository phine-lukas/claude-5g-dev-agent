#### 9.3.1.95 UE History Information

This IE contains information about cells that a UE has been served by in active state prior to the target cell.

| &gt;Last Visited Cell Information | M |  | 9.3.1.96 |  |

|-----------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
