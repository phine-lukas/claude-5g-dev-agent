## 9.9 Plain 5GS NAS message

This IE includes a complete plain 5GS NAS message as specified in clauses 8.2 and 8.3. The SECURITY PROTECTED 5GS NAS MESSAGE (see clause 8.2.28) includes a complete plain 5GS NAS message as specified in clauses 8.2. The SECURITY PROTECTED 5GS NAS MESSAGE message (see clause 8.2.28) is not plain 5GS NAS messages and shall not be included in this IE.
