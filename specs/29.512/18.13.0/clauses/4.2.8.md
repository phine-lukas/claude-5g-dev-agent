### 4.2.8 UE IP address support

As specified in 3GPP TS 23.501 \[2\], the following types of UE IP addresses may be assigned to a PDU session and may be received by the PCF:

\- IPv4 address; and/or

\- /64 IPv6 Prefix; or

\- IPv6 prefix shorter than the default /64 prefix when IPv6 Prefix Delegation applies.

More than one UE IP addresses may be assigned to a PDU session, as specified in clause 4.2.4.11.
