#### 9.2.3.64 UE History Information

The *UE History Information* IE contains information about cells that a UE has been served by in RRC_CONNECTED state prior to the target cell. The overall mechanism is described in TS 38.300 \[9\].

NOTE: The definition of this IE is aligned with the definition of the *UE History Information* IE in TS 38.413 \[5\].
